Each plot at The District comes with an electronic A khata, and the project is approved by all major banks for plot loans.

E-khata for every plot

Each plot at The District comes with an electronic A khata (e-khata). The khata is the property's entry in the local authority's records. Lenders ask for it when you take a loan, and you need it when you apply for a building plan.

Bank loans

The project is approved by all major banks for plot loans. Loan terms depend on your lender and your eligibility.

The EMI calculator on the home page works out a monthly figure from the indicative total of any plot, with your own down payment, interest rate and tenure.
