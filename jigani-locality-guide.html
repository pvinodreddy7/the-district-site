Locality guide

Jigani locality guide for plot buyers

What is around The District today, what is being built, and how long it takes to reach the places that matter.

Where Jigani is

Jigani is in Anekal Taluk, on the southern side of Bengaluru. The District is at Nosennuru Gollahalli in Jigani Hobli (PIN 562106), in the Jigani and Bommasandra corridor.

The cricket stadium, about 1.5 km away

An international cricket stadium with a planned capacity of 80,000 is being built at Suryanagar 4th Phase in Anekal taluk. The foundation stone was laid on 23 May 2026. A foundation stone is the start of a project, so treat the stadium as a long-term factor. Read about the stadium.

The Jigani industrial area

Jigani and Bommasandra are joined by a link road of about 6 to 7 km that connects through to Electronic City. Companies reported along it include Biocon, Cipla, Micro Labs, Mylan Laboratories and HCL Technologies. The people who work here are the natural buyers and tenants for homes in the area.

It is worth being candid: residents and businesses have complained about potholes, lighting and drainage on the link road. Read about the corridor.

Electronic City and the metro

Electronic City is a 25 to 30 minute drive. The Yellow Line metro is running to Bommasandra, with the station 15 to 20 minutes away. An extension towards Attibele is being planned, and the state has said it will consider a link towards Jigani.

Also in the area

  • Satellite Town Ring Road: the alignment passes 1.7 km to the south. The southern packages have been tendered and are not open yet.
  • IIM Bangalore's second campus at Mahanthalingapura, Jigani, with its first 80 undergraduates.
  • Bannerghatta National Park, a protected forest with a zoo and safaris.

Based on public announcements as of November 2026. Timelines for public projects can change, and past price trends do not guarantee future prices.

Quick answers

How far is the new cricket stadium from The District?

The stadium site at Suryanagar 4th Phase is about 1.5 km from The District.

How long does it take to reach Electronic City from The District?

About 25 to 30 minutes by road, depending on traffic.

Is there a metro near Jigani?

The Yellow Line is running to Bommasandra, 15 to 20 minutes from The District. An extension towards Attibele is being planned.
