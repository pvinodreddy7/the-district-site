BMRDA approved plots in Jigani

The District by Navilu is a 226-plot layout sanctioned by the Anekal Planning Authority, which works under BMRDA, and registered with Karnataka RERA.

What "BMRDA approved" means in Jigani

Jigani is in Anekal Taluk. Layouts here are sanctioned by the Anekal Planning Authority, the local planning authority under the Bangalore Metropolitan Region Development Authority (BMRDA). The layout plan of The District was sanctioned in May 2025.

Download the approved layout plan (PDF, 1.5 MB) and check the plot numbers, road widths and park areas for yourself.

What the approved plan shows

Less than 52% of the land is sold as plots. The rest is roads, parks and civic amenity sites.

The layout covers 57,768 sq m (about 14.3 acres) with 226 plots. Internal roads are 9 m (30 ft) wide. A 30 m (100 ft) master plan road runs through the layout, on a strip the approved plan keeps free of plots.

How to check it yourself

  • Open the approved plan and match it with the live master plan.
  • Look up the RERA number on rera.karnataka.gov.in.
  • Walk the layout. Roads, drains, electricity and water are complete, so what you see on the plan is on the ground.

Quick answers

Who approved the layout of The District?

The Anekal Planning Authority, the local planning authority under BMRDA, sanctioned the layout plan in May 2025.

Is The District RERA registered?

Yes. The Karnataka RERA registration number is PRM/KA/RERA/1251/308/PR/250825/008029.

How much of the layout is open space?

Parks take up 10.48% of the land, across 13 parks covering 6,052 sq m. Roads take 33.25% and civic amenity sites 5.01%.
