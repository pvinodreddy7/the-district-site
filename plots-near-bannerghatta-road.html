Plots near Bannerghatta Road, in Jigani

For buyers looking along Bannerghatta Road: an approved plotted layout further down the same belt, where land is still sold by the plot.

Why Bannerghatta Road buyers look at Jigani

Bannerghatta Road runs south from the city past the national park and on towards Jigani and Anekal. Further down the belt, in Jigani, you can buy an individual plot in an approved layout and build your own home.

The District by Navilu is one such layout: 226 villa plots at Nosennuru Gollahalli, Jigani Hobli, sanctioned by the Anekal Planning Authority (BMRDA) and registered with Karnataka RERA.

The forest next door

Bannerghatta National Park lies in the Anekal hills and was declared a national park in 1974. Bannerghatta Biological Park, with its zoo, safaris and butterfly park, covers about 732 hectares.

The park is ringed by an eco-sensitive zone where certain activities are restricted. For buyers anywhere near the park boundary, that is a reason to check that a layout has its approvals in place. The District has its layout approval and RERA registration, and the approved plan is open to download. Read about the park.

IIM Bangalore at both ends

IIM Bangalore's main campus is on Bannerghatta Road. Its second campus, of about 110 acres, is at Mahanthalingapura in Jigani, roughly 27 to 30 km from the main one. The first undergraduate batch has 80 students, projected to grow to 640 by 2031. Read about the campus.
