How to buy a plot in Bangalore, step by step

Seven steps from first shortlist to a registered sale deed, with what to check and what to budget at each one.

The seven steps

StepWhat happens
1. ShortlistDecide the side of the city, the budget and the plot size. Visit on a weekday and drive the commute you will actually make.
2. Check the papersLayout approval, RERA registration, land conversion, title and khata. Use the six-point checklist.
3. Pick the plotLook at size, facing, road width and what is next to it: a park, a main road, a corner.
4. Get the cost sheetAsk for one written figure: plot cost, fixed charges, and an estimate of stamp duty and registration.
5. BookPay the booking amount and get a receipt and an agreement that names the plot number, area and payment schedule.
6. Arrange the moneyIf you are taking a loan, the lender does its own legal and technical check before it releases funds. See plot loan vs home loan.
7. RegisterThe sale deed is registered at the Sub-Registrar's office after stamp duty and the registration fee are paid. Then the khata is transferred to your name.

What to budget beyond the plot price

  • Stamp duty and the registration fee, which together come to several percent of the value
  • Any fixed project charges, such as a clubhouse charge
  • Loan processing fees, if you borrow
  • Later, the cost of building: plan approval, construction and utility connections

Three mistakes to avoid

  • Booking from a brochure without walking the layout. Check that the roads, drains, power and water are actually there.
  • Trusting a photocopy. Download the approved plan and look up the RERA number yourself.
  • Budgeting only for the plot. Statutory charges are paid on top, at registration.

Quick answers

When do I pay stamp duty on a plot?

At registration. Stamp duty and the registration fee are paid before the sale deed is registered at the Sub-Registrar's office.

Can I build as soon as I buy the plot?

Once your sale deed is registered you can apply for building plan approval for your plot.
