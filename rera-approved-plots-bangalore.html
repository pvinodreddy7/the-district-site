Open space is worth checking too

Ask how much of the land is actually sold as plots. At The District it is less than 52%. Roads take 33.25%, 13 parks take 10.48% and civic amenity sites 5.01%, all from the approved plan.

Where The District is

The District by Navilu is in Jigani, in Anekal Taluk on the southern side of Bengaluru. Electronic City is 25 to 30 minutes away and the Bommasandra Metro station 15 to 20 minutes. It is a long way from the airport in the north of the city, so it suits buyers whose work and family are on the southern side.

Drive times are approximate and depend on traffic.

The price, in one line

One per sq ft rate across the layout, plus a ₹1.5 lakh clubhouse charge per plot. Stamp duty, registration and other statutory charges are paid separately. See the current rate with the cost estimator.
