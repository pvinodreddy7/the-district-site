The 80,000-seat cricket stadium coming up at Suryanagar

Karnataka has started work on what is planned as India's second-largest cricket stadium, in Anekal taluk.

An international cricket stadium with a planned capacity of 80,000 is being built at Suryanagar 4th Phase in Anekal taluk, on the south-eastern edge of Bengaluru. The Chief Minister laid the foundation stone on 23 May 2026.

Once complete it is expected to be the second-largest cricket stadium in the country, after the Narendra Modi Stadium in Ahmedabad. The project is being developed and funded by the Karnataka Housing Board, without a direct allocation from the state budget.

The stadium itself has been estimated at about ₹943 crore. It sits within a wider sports complex that was announced in 2025 at about ₹1,650 crore, with facilities for other indoor and outdoor sports.

Large venues tend to pull roads, public transport, hotels and retail towards them over time. That said, a foundation stone is the start of a project, not the end of one. Timelines for projects of this size often move, so treat the stadium as a long-term factor.

What it means for The District

The District is ~1.5 km from the stadium site. For a plot buyer, that means being close to a major public investment while living on an internal road, off the main traffic.
