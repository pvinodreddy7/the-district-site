IIM Bangalore's second campus in Jigani

One of India's best-known management schools is building its undergraduate campus in the Jigani and Anekal belt.

IIM Bangalore is developing a second campus of about 110 acres at Mahanthalingapura, Jigani, roughly 27 to 30 km from its main campus on Bannerghatta Road. The first building there, a management development centre, was inaugurated in March 2023.

The campus is the home of the institute's first undergraduate programmes: four-year BSc (Honours) degrees in Economics and in Data Science. The first batch has 80 students, 40 in each programme, starting in the 2026 to 2027 academic year.

The institute has said the cohort is projected to grow to 640 students by 2031 as more of the campus is built. In August 2026 it signed an agreement with Tata Trusts to establish the undergraduate school, which supports the development of the new campus.

A residential campus of this standing brings faculty, staff, students and visiting families into the area, along with the housing, food and services they need.

What it means for The District

The District is in the same Jigani belt as the new campus. An anchor institution like this adds a long-term source of demand to an area better known for industry.
