The Satellite Town Ring Road and the Hosur, Anekal and Kanakapura stretch

What the ring road is, what is open today, and what is coming on the southern side.

The Satellite Town Ring Road (STRR, NH 948A) is a 280.8 km access-controlled highway being built by the National Highways Authority of India around Bengaluru. It is planned to connect 12 satellite towns, including Hoskote, Sarjapura, Attibele, Anekal, Kanakapura, Ramanagara and Magadi, and to extend to Hosur in Tamil Nadu.

Its main purpose is to let freight and inter-state traffic bypass the city. An 80 km section from Dobbaspet to Hoskote was inaugurated on 11 March 2024.

The Hoskote to Hosur stretch has been delayed by a railway overbridge. Recent reports say a 21 km section may open by December, with the overbridge work running until March 2027.

On the southern and western side, running from Hosur through Anekal and Kanakapura towards Ramanagara, the remaining packages have been tendered, including one from Thattekere in Kanakapura taluk to Bagganadoddi in Anekal taluk. This part is not open yet, and dates have slipped before, so buyers should treat its timing as uncertain.

What it means for The District

When the southern stretch is built, the Anekal belt gets a direct, signal-free route towards Hosur on one side and Kanakapura and Mysuru Road on the other, without going through the city.
