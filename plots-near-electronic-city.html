Plots near Electronic City

Drive times are approximate and depend on traffic.

The Bommasandra and Jigani link road, about 6 to 7 km long, connects the Jigani side through to Electronic City. To be candid, residents and businesses have complained about potholes, lighting and drainage on this road, so everyday comfort depends on continued civic work.

Metro today, and what is planned

The Yellow Line is running to Bommasandra. An extension towards Attibele is being planned, and the state has said it will consider a link towards Jigani.

Employers on this side of Electronic City

Companies reported along the link road include Biocon, Cipla, Micro Labs, Mylan Laboratories and HCL Technologies. A Deccan Herald report noted that the industrial estates of Bommasandra, Jigani, Attibele and Electronics City, together more than 3,300 acres, have run out of space. Read about the corridor.

A plot of your own

The District by Navilu has 226 villa plots, mostly 30 × 40 ft (1,200 sq ft) and 30 × 50 ft (1,500 sq ft). The layout is sanctioned by the Anekal Planning Authority (BMRDA) and registered with Karnataka RERA. Roads, drains, electricity and water are complete, and sale deeds are being registered.
