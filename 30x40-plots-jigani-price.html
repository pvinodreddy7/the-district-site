Standard plots are 30 × 40 ft and 30 × 50 ft. Plots open for booking currently range from 1,052 to 2,192 sq ft. Internal roads are 9 m (30 ft) wide.

How the price is worked out

There is one per sq ft rate across the layout, plus a fixed clubhouse charge of ₹1.5 lakh per plot.

Plot area in sq ft × rate per sq ft + ₹1,50,000 clubhouse charge = indicative total

Stamp duty, registration fees and other statutory charges are paid separately at registration. Ask for the cost sheet for the final figure.

See the current rate for your plot

The cost estimator on the home page shows the current rate once you enter your name and mobile number. Pick an available plot, see its indicative total, and work out the EMI for your down payment, interest rate and tenure.

You can also choose a plot straight from the live master plan, which shows what is available and what is booked.

What comes with the plot

  • Electronic A khata (e-khata) for each plot
  • Approved by all major banks for plot loans
  • Roads, drains, electricity and water complete
  • Sale deeds being registered for booked plots
  • A clubhouse, swimming pool, pickleball courts, a Miyawaki forest and tropical themed parks being built for residents

Quick answers

How many sq ft is a 30x40 plot?

A 30 × 40 ft plot is 1,200 sq ft, or about 111.5 sq m. A 30 × 50 ft plot is 1,500 sq ft, or about 139.4 sq m.

Is the rate the same for every plot?

Yes. There is one per sq ft rate across the layout, plus a clubhouse charge of ₹1.5 lakh per plot.

What is not included in the price?

Stamp duty, registration fees and other statutory charges are paid separately at registration.
