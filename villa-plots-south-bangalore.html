Rental demand

Homes here have a ready tenant base: the pharma and technology employers on the Jigani and Bommasandra link road, and the students, faculty and staff of IIM Bangalore's Jigani campus.

The layout

The District by Navilu is a gated villa plot development of 226 plots on about 14.3 acres, with a tropical theme. Less than 52% of the land is sold as plots. The rest is roads, 13 parks and civic amenity sites. It is sanctioned by the Anekal Planning Authority (BMRDA), registered with Karnataka RERA, and the infrastructure is complete.

Quick answers

Where in South Bangalore is The District?

At Nosennuru Gollahalli, Jigani Hobli, Anekal Taluk, Karnataka 562106, in the Jigani and Bommasandra corridor.

What infrastructure is coming up near The District?

An 80,000-seat cricket stadium about 1.5 km away, the Satellite Town Ring Road 1.7 km to the south, a planned metro extension towards Attibele, and the growth of IIM Bangalore's Jigani campus.
